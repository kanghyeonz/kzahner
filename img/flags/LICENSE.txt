kr.svg, ch.svg, fr.svg, gb.svg and de.svg are the waved region flags from the Noto Emoji
project (https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji, directory
third_party/region-flags/waved-svg). According to that project, the flag
images under third_party/region-flags are in the public domain or otherwise
exempt from copyright. The outline drawn around each flag has been removed
here; the page adds a soft shadow instead.
